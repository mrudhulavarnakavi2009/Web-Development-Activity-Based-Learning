About Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is a beautiful state in southeastern India. It is known for its rich culture, historic monuments, spiritual temples, beaches, hills and rivers.

From the green hills of Araku Valley to the beautiful beaches of Visakhapatnam, the state offers many wonderful destinations for travellers.

About Explore AP

Explore AP is a travel and tourism website created to introduce the beautiful destinations of Andhra Pradesh.

Our website provides information about popular tourist places, travel packages and beautiful locations to help visitors plan their journeys.

Our aim is to encourage people to explore new places and appreciate the natural beauty and culture of Andhra Pradesh.
